JustMD

# Markdown,
without the noise.

A native Mac editor for your .md files. Syntax appears on the line you edit, then gets out of the way when you move on.

## From a file to a finished page.

Write a note. Read a README. Work through your list.

01 / WRITE

See what you’re writing.

Headings look like headings. Bold reads as bold. Move to a line to see its Markdown and edit it.

02 / READ

Settle into the document.

Press ⇧⌘E for Read mode, with table grids, local images, and clickable links. Print the rendered page or save it as a PDF.

03 / CHECK OFF

A little more done.

Click a checkbox to finish a task. Choose a built-in theme or make a palette of your own.

## Your files stay yours.

Open an existing file from Finder or drop it onto JustMD. Save it right where it belongs, and open it in another editor whenever you like.

  • - [x] Plain Markdown files on your Mac.
  • - [x] Native document windows, autosave, and macOS Versions.
  • - [x] Free and open source. No subscription or account required.
