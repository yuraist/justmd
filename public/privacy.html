Privacy Policy

The JustMD app

JustMD does not collect any data. Effective date: June 11, 2026.

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers.
  • Your documents never leave your Mac. JustMD has no server and makes no network requests with your content.
  • The app runs inside the macOS App Sandbox and can only read files you explicitly open or drop on it.
  • Preferences (theme, font size) are stored locally on your device.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to share, sell, or delete. If this policy ever changes, the change will be listed here and in the app's release notes first.

This website

Updated September 15, 2026. Download links count requests by destination (App Store or DMG) and button position (top or bottom of the page), using Cloudflare Analytics Engine. The event contains no IP address, user identifier, user agent, or referrer. These counts measure download intent, not installations or unique people.

The website does not set analytics cookies. Cloudflare processes requests to serve the website and download redirects. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Your Show syntax preference is saved only in your browser’s local storage.
